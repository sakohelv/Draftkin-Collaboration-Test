## Deliverables and deadlines

In **Project overview → Tasks & deadlines**, select a submission deadline to open its week. Accepted deliverables keep their due date on the calendar. The project overview uses issued decisions to identify completed work and the next assigned stage. **Issued presentations** shows the shared issue and decision. **My editable presentations** identifies the local source, including references shared with invitations.

## Receiving files

Open **Document Exchange → Received → Files** for files, with their sender, purpose and date. Choose **View package** to follow an issue through feedback and later versions. **Package history** lists deliveries newest first. Within a package, **Submission history** shows related issues and responses oldest first.

## Internal review

In a PDF markup, choose **Request a revision** or **For information**, then **Prepare response**. An editor can **Send for internal review**. A manager can open **Studio team → Needs internal review**, return the draft to the editor, or approve and send it. A manager preparing the response can send it directly. Use **View internal reviews** in the presentation editor for reviews within your team.
