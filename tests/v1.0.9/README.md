## Invite a Lighting consultant

1. Register the manager’s email in **Studio Test**. Create a project, choose its stages and save its scope and deliverables.
2. Register a separate consultant email in **Lighting Test**. The account belongs to the test app where it was registered.
3. In Studio, open the project's **Invite discipline** form, choose **Lighting**, and enter the consultant email. Choose the stages, attach a presentation if needed, then select **Send invitation**.
4. In Lighting, open **Invitations** and accept. Open the project overview to see the shared brief, scope and assigned deliverables.

The form identifies a project owner or internal teammate entered as an external consultant. Correct the email in the same form to keep your stage and presentation selections. Invitations to a new address remain available after registration and follow the existing account confirmation workflow.
