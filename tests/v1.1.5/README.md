## Check an existing appointment

In Studio, open your project and choose **Scope & team → Disciplines & invitations**. A team that has accepted shows **Connected**. Choose **View appointment** to see the consultant and the available actions. Pending invitations show **Invitation pending** or **Account confirmation needed** when the project lead has a confirmation to review.

For a new discipline, choose **Invite consultant**, select the stages, and send once. The consultant accepts in their app. For an online project, subsequent scope and deliverable changes saved in the originating Studio workspace are shared with that team for its assigned stages. Use **Document Exchange** when you want to issue files.

When the appointment service is unavailable, Studio shows **Status unavailable**. Choose **Reload** to check again.
