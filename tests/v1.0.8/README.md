## In Studio

1. Save the project brief and project scope in Project setup. Save the scopes and deliverables for each discipline and stage in Scope & team.
2. Open **Project team → Invite discipline**, choose the discipline, and enter the recipient’s account email. Review the assigned stages, scope and deliverables shown in the invitation. You can also attach a presentation.
3. Click **Send invitation**. The recipient accepts in their test app. Saving changes to the assigned work in Studio updates the connected assignment. An issued presentation keeps its original contents.

## In the discipline app

Open the project and choose **Project overview** for the project brief, general scope and discipline assignment. **Stages & programme** shows what is due at each assigned stage, including the requirements and any deliverable dates Studio recorded.

Open **Document Exchange → Submission milestones** to prepare a submission against an assigned deliverable. Presentations attached to the invitation are available under **Received**.

Lighting consultants use Lighting Test. Other appointed disciplines use their consultant workspace in Studio Test.
