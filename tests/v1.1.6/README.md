## Return a marked presentation to Studio

1. In Lighting, open the project and choose **Presentations → Reviews**. Find the presentation under **Returned markups** and choose **Revise & send back**. You can also open the review directly from its working presentation card.
2. Read Studio’s marked PDF on the left and edit the original slides on the right. For an older issue with no recorded slide order, select the matching slide in the editor.
3. Choose **Preview Issue 02** to check the revised PDF. A project member with approval and issue permissions can choose **Approve & send Issue 02 to Studio**. Other authorized authors choose **Request approval for Issue 02**. The new issue appears in the existing Document Exchange history.

The editable draft must be available on the authoring computer. A teammate who only has the issued PDF can open and read the markup.

## Delete a presentation

In either app, open **Presentations** and choose **Delete presentation** on a card you own. Confirm the deletion in the prompt. Previously issued copies remain available in the project’s exchange history.
