## Stage completion

Open **Stages & programme** and expand a stage. Shared projects show the assigned deliverable names, requirements, due dates and available submission status. Acceptance follows the latest linked issue in each submission series. To submit work against an assigned deliverable, open **Submission milestones**, find the deliverable and choose **Prepare submission**. Stage targets and deliverable due dates can differ.

## Delete a presentation you own

Open **Create & edit → All presentations**. On your working presentation choose **Delete presentation** and confirm. This works for presentations built in the editor and those that contain imported pages. Previously issued packages and review history keep their copies. Your own uploaded PDF versions also offer deletion under **Published PDF versions**, subject to existing published-document controls.

## Mark up a PDF from Lighting

In Studio, open **Document Exchange → Received**, open the package and choose **Preview → Mark up PDF**. Use the markup toolbar to draw or add comments on any page. Closing the markup window keeps your draft on that computer. **Download marked PDF** exports a copy with the marks and notes.

With document editing access, **Prepare response** creates a linked response draft containing the marked PDF. Request approval and issue it through Document Exchange to return it to Lighting. The review decision on the original issue remains a separate action.
